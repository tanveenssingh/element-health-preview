Good to know

Common questions

How long before bed should I take the oil?

Most customers take their serving 30 to 60 minutes before they want to fall asleep. Holding it under the tongue for a minute before swallowing helps it absorb.

Can I take the oil and the CBN gummies together?

Yes. The oil is the evening serving and the CBN gummy is there if you wake in the night. Many customers use only one of the two; start with the oil and add the gummy if you need it.

Is there THC in these?

The CBN Nighttime Gummies are THC free. The full spectrum oils, softgels and gummies contain no more than 0.3% Δ9-THC on a dry-weight basis, as permitted under the 2018 Farm Bill. Full spectrum products can show up on a drug test.

How do I know which size to buy?

The 15 mL is a two to three week trial at typical servings. The 60 mL is the best value per mg and lasts most people several months.
