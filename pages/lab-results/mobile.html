1
Who tested it

An independent lab, not us. The top of every report names the lab, its address and its accreditation. Badger Labs reports to the ISO/IEC 17025:2017 standard for testing labs.

2
Which batch

The sample name and batch ID tie the results to one production run. Match it to the number on your label.

3
What’s inside

Each cannabinoid is listed by % weight and by amount, in mg per gummy or mg per gram of oil. The amounts should line up with what the label promises.

4
How much THC

Labs report total THC as Δ9-THC plus 0.877 × THCA, the amount THCA turns into when heated. ND means none was detected at the lab’s limit of quantitation (LOQ).

Good to know

Lab result questions

What is a certificate of analysis?

A COA is a report from a testing lab that lists what’s in a product sample and how much. Ours come from independent labs, so the results aren’t ours to edit.

Why do full spectrum products contain THC?

Full spectrum extracts keep the hemp plant’s natural range of cannabinoids, which includes small amounts of Δ9-THC. Each certificate shows exactly how much. If you’re drug tested, look at our THC-free formulas: Broad Spectrum, CBN Sleep and Energy & Focus gummies.

Can a THC-free product affect a drug test?

Our THC-free gummies show no detectable THC on their certificates. Drug tests vary, though, so no hemp product can promise a particular result.

My batch number doesn’t match the one shown.

We post the most recent certificate for each product. Email us the batch number from your label and we’ll send the certificate for that run.

What do ND and LOQ mean?

ND means not detected. LOQ, the limit of quantitation, is the smallest amount the lab’s method can measure reliably. A result below it is reported as ND or <LOQ.
