1.About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Element Health, LLC (“Element Health,” “we,” “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on elementhealthsupply.com (the “Site”). Please read it together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information.

2.What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They let the site remember things like what is in your cart or the choices you have made, and help us understand how the site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in much the same way. In this policy we call all of them “cookies.”

Some cookies last only until you close your browser (session cookies). Others stay on your device for a set period or until you delete them (persistent cookies).

3.How we use cookies

Strictly necessary

These cookies make the Site work. They keep items in your cart, run checkout and your account, help prevent fraud, remember your age confirmation and store your cookie choices. The Site cannot work properly without them, so they cannot be switched off.

Functionality

These cookies remember your preferences, such as settings you choose in our accessibility tool, so you don’t have to set them again.

Analytics

These cookies tell us how visitors use the Site, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay and how they found us. We use this to improve the Site.

Email

When you sign up for our emails or click a link in one, cookies help us recognize you on the Site, so we don’t show you a sign-up form you’ve already filled in and so we can see which emails are useful.

We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies.

4.Who sets cookies on our Site

  • Shopify, which runs our store, cart, checkout and cookie banner.
  • Google Analytics, which measures how the Site is used.
  • Klaviyo, which runs our email sign-up forms and email measurement.
  • Stamped, which shows and collects product reviews.
  • Our accessibility tool, which remembers your accessibility settings.

Each of these providers handles information under its own privacy policy.

5.Managing cookies

You can accept or decline optional cookies in our cookie banner and change your choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; your browser’s help section explains how. To stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits on any website, you can install Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the Site, such as your cart and checkout, will not work.

6.Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when the tools we use change. We will post the updated version here with a new “Last updated” date.
