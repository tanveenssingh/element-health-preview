1.Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Element Health, LLC (“Element Health,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit elementhealthsupply.com (the “Site”), buy from us, or contact us. Element Health is based in Surfside, Florida, and ships to customers in the United States and Canada.

By using the Site you agree to the practices described here. If you have questions, contact us at info@elementhealthsupply.com.

2.Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping and billing address.
  • Order details, such as the products you buy, order history and any notes you add at checkout.
  • Account details, if you create an account, such as your login email and saved addresses.
  • Messages you send us through our contact form, by email or on social media.
  • Reviews, ratings and photos you choose to submit about our products.
  • Confirmation that you meet our minimum age requirement.

Payment information

Payments are handled by Shopify and our payment processors. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Site we and our service providers collect device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, pages viewed, referring site, and how you interact with the Site and our emails. We collect this using cookies and similar technologies (see Cookies and tracking below).

3.How we use your information

  • To process, ship and support your orders, including sending order and shipping updates.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To send marketing emails if you have signed up or bought from us, where the law allows. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To collect and display product reviews.
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Site and checkout secure.
  • To confirm age eligibility and meet our legal and tax obligations.

4.How we share your information

We share personal information only as needed to run our business, with:

  • Shopify, which hosts our store and checkout.
  • Payment processors, to take payment and prevent fraud.
  • Shipping carriers such as USPS, to deliver your order.
  • Klaviyo, which sends our emails.
  • Stamped, which collects and displays product reviews.
  • Analytics and site tools, such as Google Analytics and our accessibility widget.
  • Professional advisers, and authorities when the law requires it or to protect our rights, our customers or others.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganized.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5.Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to keep the Site and your cart working, remember your preferences, and measure how the Site is used. You can manage cookies through our cookie banner and your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Site works.

6.Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information or targeted advertising. We do neither, but you may still ask us to confirm.
  • Appeal a decision we make about your request.

To make a request, email info@elementhealthsupply.com. We may need to verify your identity before we act on it. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email.

7.How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide our products and services, keep business and tax records, resolve disputes and meet our legal obligations. When we no longer need it, we delete or de-identify it.

8.Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, and our store and checkout run on Shopify’s secure platform. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9.Age requirement

The Site and our products are intended only for adults who meet our minimum age requirement of 21. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21. If you believe a minor has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10.Where information is processed

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you are in Canada, your information may be processed outside Canada and may be accessible to authorities there under local law.

11.Links to other sites

The Site may link to other websites and social media pages. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new “Last updated” date.
