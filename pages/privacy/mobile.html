1.Introduction

Element Health sells CBD-based products to clients located around the world. At Element Health, the privacy and security of our customers and visitors are of paramount importance. Element Health is committed to protecting the data you share with us. This privacy policy explains how Element Health processes information that can be used to directly or indirectly identify an individual (“Personal Data”) collected through use of its website.

Any information stored by Element Health is treated as confidential. All information is stored securely and is accessed by authorized personnel only. Element Health implements and maintains appropriate technical, security and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or unlawful processing and use, and against accidental loss, destruction, damage, theft or disclosure.
