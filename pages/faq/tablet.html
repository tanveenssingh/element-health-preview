About CBD

4 questions
What is CBD?

Cannabidiol, also known as CBD, is a compound derived from the hemp plant. It does not cause the “high” or “stoned” feeling associated with THC. It is usually found in concentrated oil form which can be taken orally or applied topically.

What are the benefits of CBD?

CBD works with the body’s Endocannabinoid System (ECS), which helps the body maintain homeostasis, or internal balance. Full spectrum formulas, which contain a range of cannabinoids, terpenes and other plant compounds, are what many of our customers use to support restful sleep, a calmer response to everyday stress, and recovery after activity. Research into CBD is ongoing and everyone responds differently, so we suggest starting with a low serving and adjusting over time.

Is CBD right for me?

Our products are made for adults 21 and over who want to add hemp-derived CBD to their wellness routine. If you are pregnant or nursing, have a medical condition or take prescription medication, talk with your doctor before use. Many customers also share CBD with their pets through our Pure Pet CBD Oil.

Why choose Element Health?

Our Full Spectrum CBD oil is extracted from organically grown hemp on 7th-generation family farms. It features a robust profile of active cannabinoids and is made to support everyday wellness. Grown in nutrient-rich soil and produced using meticulous extraction techniques, our oil is high in potency and verified by third-party lab testing. Plus, our dedicated customer support team makes you feel like part of the Element Health family. All products and services are based in the USA.
